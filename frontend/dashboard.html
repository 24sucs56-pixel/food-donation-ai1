Help Center & Support

How do food donations work?

Donors post available food items. AI assesses freshness and safety. Nearby verified NGOs or Volunteers claim and pick up food for delivery.

How to update profile or credentials?

Click your profile picture in the top right corner and select Edit Profile to change name, phone, address, or avatar. Select Change Password to update security credentials.
